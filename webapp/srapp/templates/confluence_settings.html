How publishing works

  1. Reports → an execution → Confluence.
  2. The form is pre-filled from the JMeter HTML report (statistics.json) and InfluxDB for that execution window.
  3. Charts are rendered as PNG and uploaded as page attachments together with the zipped HTML report, the .jmx and the console log.
  4. Publishing again updates the same page (new version).

The Master needs outbound HTTPS to Confluence (NAT Gateway or HTTPS_PROXY in the service environment).
